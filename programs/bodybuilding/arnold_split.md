# Arnold Split

The Arnold Split reflects the training philosophy of Arnold Schwarzenegger's prime competitive years (1970–1975). The key insight is pairing antagonist muscle groups — chest with back, shoulders with arms — which allows natural superset organization: while the chest is being pressed, the back recovers; while the biceps are being curled, the triceps recover. This allows higher total volume per session without compromising quality, and the twice-per-week frequency for each muscle group aligns with modern evidence on optimal training frequency for hypertrophy.

## Who This Is For

- Intermediate trainees who have completed a full-body or upper-lower program and want to increase training frequency and volume
- Athletes interested in the Golden Era aesthetic: broad shoulders, full chest, narrow waist, and proportional development
- Those who can commit to 6 training days per week (the program does not function on 5 or fewer days)

## Weekly Schedule

| Day | Focus | Key Movement Pairing |
|-----|-------|---------------------|
| Monday | Chest + Back | Bench ↔ Row (antagonist superset) |
| Tuesday | Shoulders + Arms | Press ↔ Curl+Extension |
| Wednesday | Legs | Squat → Leg Press → Isolation |
| Thursday | Chest + Back | Same as Monday (heavier or varied) |
| Friday | Shoulders + Arms | Same as Tuesday (heavier or varied) |
| Saturday | Legs | Same as Wednesday |
| Sunday | Rest | — |

## Arnold's Training Principles

1. **Mind-muscle connection**: Visualize the target muscle contracting with each repetition; mental focus increases EMG activation of the intended muscle
2. **Full range of motion**: Partial reps shortchange the stretch-mediated hypertrophic stimulus; Arnold always insisted on full ROM
3. **Shock the muscle periodically**: Vary training order, angles, or loading scheme every 4–6 weeks to prevent accommodation
4. **Priority training**: Train weakest body parts first in the session when CNS freshness is maximal

## Progression

- **Compound lifts**: Linear load increase when all reps are achieved with good form
- **Isolation lifts**: Double-progression (advance within rep range, then add load)
- **Every 4 weeks**: Rotate one exercise per muscle group to introduce novel stimulus
- **Deload (Week 9)**: 50% volume for one week
