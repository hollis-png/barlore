# PHUL — Power Hypertrophy Upper Lower

Developed by Dr. Layne Norton. Combines low-rep strength work (power days) with high-rep hypertrophy work across a four-day upper/lower split, targeting both neural adaptations and metabolic hypertrophy simultaneously.

## Weekly Schedule

| Day | Session | Rep Range | Focus |
|-----|---------|-----------|-------|
| Monday | Upper Power | 3–5 | Strength / neural |
| Tuesday | Lower Power | 3–5 | Strength / neural |
| Wednesday | Rest | — | — |
| Thursday | Upper Hypertrophy | 8–12 | Volume / metabolic |
| Friday | Lower Hypertrophy | 8–15 | Volume / metabolic |
| Sat/Sun | Rest | — | — |

## Day 1 — Upper Power

| Exercise | Sets × Reps |
|----------|-------------|
| Bench Press | 3-4 × 3-5 |
| Incline Dumbbell Press | 3-4 × 6-10 |
| Bent Over Barbell Row | 3-4 × 3-5 |
| Weighted Pull-ups | 2-3 × 6-8 |
| Overhead Press | 2-3 × 5-8 |
| Barbell Curl | 2-3 × 6-8 |

## Day 2 — Lower Power

| Exercise | Sets × Reps |
|----------|-------------|
| Back Squat | 3-4 × 3-5 |
| Conventional Deadlift | 3-4 × 3-5 |
| Leg Press | 3-4 × 10-15 |
| Lying Leg Curl | 2-3 × 6-8 |
| Calf Raise | 4 × 6-10 |

## Day 3 — Upper Hypertrophy

| Exercise | Sets × Reps |
|----------|-------------|
| Incline Barbell Press | 3-4 × 8-12 |
| Flat Dumbbell Press | 3-4 × 8-12 |
| Seated Cable Row | 3-4 × 8-12 |
| One-Arm Dumbbell Row | 3-4 × 8-12 |
| Dumbbell Lateral Raise | 3-4 × 8-12 |
| Triceps Pushdown | 2-3 × 8-12 |
| Dumbbell Curl | 2-3 × 8-12 |

## Day 4 — Lower Hypertrophy

| Exercise | Sets × Reps |
|----------|-------------|
| Front Squat or Hack Squat | 3-4 × 8-12 |
| Romanian Deadlift | 3-4 × 8-12 |
| Leg Press | 3-4 × 12-15 |
| Leg Extension | 3-4 × 12-15 |
| Lying Leg Curl | 3-4 × 12-15 |
| Seated Calf Raise | 4 × 8-12 |

## Progression

- **Power days:** +2.5–5 kg when all reps completed with clean form
- **Hypertrophy days:** add reps; increase weight when top of range reached across all sets
- **Deload:** every 4–6 weeks, cut volume and load ~40% for one week

> For higher-volume autoregulated hypertrophy, see `rp_hypertrophy`. For pure strength, see `5_3_1`.
