# 5/3/1 BBB (Boring But Big)

Created by Jim Wendler as an accessory template for his 5/3/1 strength program, BBB (Boring But Big) adds hypertrophy volume on top of the strength-focused main work. The genius of BBB is its simplicity: the main lift follows the 5/3/1 wave (building strength and neural efficiency); the supplemental 5×10 on the same lift at 50% of Training Max (TM) provides the metabolic stress and muscle damage required for hypertrophy. Two different adaptations, one lift, one session.

## Who This Is For

- Intermediate strength athletes (powerlifters, Olympic lifters) who want to add muscle mass without abandoning their strength foundation
- Bodybuilders with basic strength experience who want a structured framework with clear progression rules
- Athletes who dislike excessive exercise variation and prefer training a few movements very well

## The Training Max (TM) Principle

The TM is **not** your true 1RM. It is set at **90% of your true 1RM**. This is the most common BBB mistake — setting TM at true 1RM makes all prescribed percentages too heavy, causes AMRAP sets to fail early, and generates excessive fatigue.

Example (Bench Press 1RM = 100 kg):
- TM = 100 × 0.90 = **90 kg**
- Week 1, Set 3 (85% of TM): 90 × 0.85 = **76.5 kg** — this is where the AMRAP happens

## Weekly Schedule

| Day | Main Lift | BBB Supplemental |
|-----|-----------|-----------------|
| Monday | Overhead Press (5/3/1) | Bench Press 5×10 at 50% TM |
| Tuesday | Deadlift (5/3/1) | Bent-over Row 5×10 at 50% DL TM |
| Thursday | Bench Press (5/3/1) | Overhead Press 5×10 at 50% OHP TM |
| Friday | Back Squat (5/3/1) | Deadlift 5×10 at 50% DL TM |

## The 5/3/1 Wave

| Week | Sets × Reps | %TM |
|------|------------|-----|
| 1 (5+ Week) | 5 / 5 / 5+ | 65 / 75 / 85% |
| 2 (3+ Week) | 3 / 3 / 3+ | 70 / 80 / 90% |
| 3 (1+ Week) | 5 / 3 / 1+ | 75 / 85 / 95% |
| 4 (Deload) | 5 / 5 / 5 | 40 / 50 / 60% |

"+" = AMRAP set. Minimum rep targets: Week 1 set = 5 reps, Week 2 set = 3 reps, Week 3 set = 1 rep. Beating minimum by a wide margin (10+ reps on Week 1) is expected and desirable.

## TM Progression

After completing one 4-week cycle:
- **Upper body lifts** (OHP, Bench): TM + 2.5 kg
- **Lower body lifts** (Squat, Deadlift): TM + 5 kg

Do not chase TM increases faster than this. Wendler emphasizes patience — a 2.5 kg upper body increase per month = 30 kg per year.

## Assistance Work

Wendler's BBB template prescribes "First Set Last" or simple pull/push/single-leg assistance:
- **Push**: 3–5 sets of 10–25 push-ups or dips
- **Pull**: 3–5 sets of 5–15 pull-ups or rows (most important assistance category)
- **Single-leg/core**: Lunges, step-ups, or ab work 3–5 sets

Do not over-complicate assistance. The main + supplemental work is the program; assistance is maintenance.

## What Makes BBB Work

The 5×10 at 50% TM creates metabolic fatigue and muscle damage via high volume at a submaximal load. The key insight is that hypertrophy does not require heavy loads — it requires sufficient mechanical tension and metabolic stress to stimulate satellite cell activity. 5×10 at 50% TM with 60–90 second rest provides exactly this, while preserving the ability to perform the heavy main work productively.
