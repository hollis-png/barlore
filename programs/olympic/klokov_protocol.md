# Dmitry Klokov Seminar Protocol

Dmitry Klokov, 2005 World Champion and 2008 Olympic silver medalist, developed a training methodology derived from Russian national team practices but adapted for his seminar curriculum. The system's central premise is that **technical proficiency is directly limited by positional strength**: an athlete cannot receive the bar correctly unless the muscles controlling the receiving position are strong enough to maintain it under load.

Klokov's program is characterized by:

1. **Strength-dominant accessory selection** — the Klokov Press (wide-grip behind-neck press), heavy trap work ("Trapi"), and Sots Press are used to build structural density in the upper back and shoulder girdle that no amount of competition lift practice can develop.
2. **Paused squat complexes** — back and front squat are combined in continuous sets with 3-second pauses to eliminate stretch reflex and build true positional strength at the bottom.
3. **Complex-heavy competition lift patterns** — rather than simple singles, Klokov chains exercises (muscle snatch + high-hang + floor) to accumulate technical volume while developing mechanical coordination across the full movement.
4. **Autoregulation** — on low-recovery days, intensity drops and volume shifts to accessory and positional work rather than forcing competition lift PRs.

## Who This Is For

- Advanced competitive lifters seeking to address structural deficiencies in the shoulder girdle or upper back
- Athletes whose snatch is limited by shoulder instability in the receiving position rather than pull strength
- Athletes who have completed a structured block periodization program and are looking for a high-specificity accessory framework
- Not appropriate for: athletes with shoulder impingement, thoracic outlet syndrome, or limited shoulder external rotation

## Prerequisite Mobility

The Klokov Press (behind-neck wide-grip press) requires:
- Full shoulder external rotation in the snatch grip width
- Complete thoracic extension under load
- Absence of shoulder impingement or anterior capsule tightness

Do not attempt the Klokov Press without confirming these prerequisites. The movement is contraindicated for athletes with any shoulder pathology.

## The Klokov Press — Technical Standard

- Grip width: snatch grip (index finger at ring marking or wider)
- Bar starts behind the neck at the base of the occiput
- Press strictly vertical — no forward lean, no hip extension assist
- At the top, elbows fully locked; bar directly over the mid-foot
- Descent: slow and controlled, full bar contact with upper traps at the bottom

**Any forward head position, thoracic rounding, or incomplete lockout means reducing load.**

## Key Rules

- **Trapi is not optional.** The upper trap work is the structural foundation of Klokov's system. Skipping it to save time removes the primary adaptation driver for shoulder girdle stability.
- **Autoregulate aggressively.** On days when systemic fatigue is elevated, drop to hang variations and accessory volume. Never force competition lift PRs through fatigue.
- **Positional strength before load.** The paused squat complexes are done at the heaviest load permitting full pause quality — never compromise the pause to add weight.
