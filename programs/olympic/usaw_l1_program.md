# USAW Level 1 Coaching Framework

The USA Weightlifting Level 1 framework is the standard entry point for structured Olympic lifting in the United States. Its defining feature is the **top-down technical progression**: athletes learn each lift starting from the high-hang position — the simplest catch mechanic — and progressively move the starting position toward the floor as positional competency is established. This approach prevents the accumulation of technique flaws from floor-up teaching, where the first pull is learned independently of the catch position.

The program uses **power versions** throughout the 12 weeks. The power snatch and power clean maintain identical pull mechanics to the full lifts while eliminating the depth requirement, allowing the coach and athlete to focus entirely on pull quality, extension timing, and receiving mechanics.

## Who This Is For

- Athletes completely new to Olympic lifting
- Trainees with some general barbell experience (can safely hinge and squat)
- Athletes returning after a long break who need a technique reset
- Not appropriate for: athletes who already have stable squat receiving positions

## Prerequisite Screening

Before loading the overhead squat or snatch:

| Mobility Check | Standard |
|---------------|----------|
| Wrist dorsiflexion | Flat palms on floor, arms straight |
| Shoulder elevation | Full overhead reach without lumbar compensation |
| Ankle dorsiflexion | Squat below parallel with flat foot |
| Thoracic extension | Upright torso in overhead squat at bodyweight |

Address mobility restrictions before loading, not alongside heavy loading. See `mobility_requirements`.

## Top-Down Position Progression

| Weeks | Snatch Starting Position | Clean Starting Position |
|-------|--------------------------|-------------------------|
| 1–3 | High-hang power snatch | High-hang power clean |
| 4–6 | Hang power snatch (above knee) | Hang power clean (above knee) |
| 7–9 | Hang power snatch (at knee) | Hang power clean (at knee) |
| 10–12 | Power snatch from floor | Power clean from floor |

**Advancement criterion:** Technically stable position AND consistent bar path across two consecutive sessions before advancing.

## Key Rules

- **Position before load:** Never increase weight when the receiving position is inconsistent. The bar waits; the position doesn't.
- **Session time cap:** 60–75 minutes. Exceeded regularly → reduce volume before adding load.
- **Squat versions are not introduced in this program.** After Week 12, transition to `olympic_weightlifting_beginner` for squat version introduction.

> After this program, see `olympic_weightlifting_beginner`. For supporting mobility work, see `mobility_requirements`.
