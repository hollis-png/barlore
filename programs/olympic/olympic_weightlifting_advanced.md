# Olympic Weightlifting Advanced — High-Frequency Competition Prep

Advanced Olympic weightlifting programming maximizes competition lift exposure. Unlike strength sports where the training tool (e.g., squats, pulls) can be separated from the competition expression, snatch and clean & jerk technique is so movement-specific that the primary adaptation mechanism is practicing the lifts themselves at near-maximal intensity. This program allocates six training days per week to competition lift practice and heavy squatting, with pulls and accessories serving as supplementary volume.

## Who This Is For

- Athletes competing in Olympic weightlifting at regional or national level
- Minimum baseline: Snatch ≥ 90% of body weight (male) or ≥ 80% (female); Clean & Jerk ≥ 110% of body weight (male) or ≥ 90% (female)
- Athletes with established technique on all major movements — this program volume assumes motor patterns are stable under heavy load

## 16-Week Block Structure

| Weeks | Phase | Character |
|-------|-------|-----------|
| 1–4 | Accumulation | Build volume; daily maxes at ~90% effort |
| 5–7 | Intensification | Push daily maxes higher; reduce accessory volume |
| 8 | Deload | 85% cap; no PRs |
| 9–12 | Intensification II | Heavy singles above previous bests |
| 13 | Deload | 85% cap |
| 14–15 | Peaking | Competition-openers and second attempts only |
| 16 | Competition Week | Monday: 90% and 95%. Tue–Thu: rest. Competition day. |

## Weekly Schedule

| Day | AM | PM |
|-----|----|----|
| Mon | Snatch + Back Squat | Snatch Pull + Power Clean |
| Tue | Clean & Jerk + Front Squat | Clean Pull + Push Press |
| Wed | Power Snatch + Back Squat | Overhead Squat + Good Morning |
| Thu | Snatch + Front Squat | Clean Pull + Power Clean |
| Fri | Clean & Jerk + Back Squat | Snatch Pull + Push Press |
| Sat | Power Snatch + Power Clean + Front Squat | Good Morning |
| Sun | Rest | |

Two-a-day sessions are separated by 4–6 hours minimum. PM sessions are shorter and lower intensity than AM sessions.

## Session Structure (AM — Competition Lift Day)

1. General warm-up: 10 min light rowing or bike
2. Movement warm-up: 3–4 sets with empty bar → 50% → 70% → 80%
3. Work to daily max (typically 3–5 progressive singles)
4. Back-off sets: 2–3 singles at 80–85% of daily max
5. Supplementary compound (squat or pull)

## Competition Preparation

**Openers**: select at 90–92% of best in training for each lift.
**Second attempts**: 97–100% of personal best.
**Third attempts**: PR or conservative +1 kg over previous best.

Attempt selection is the most critical non-training factor in competition performance. Conservative openers minimize missed lifts and allow full expression of fitness on the platform.
