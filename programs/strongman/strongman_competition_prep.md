# Strongman Competition Preparation

Advanced strongman programming is contest-driven: the contest date defines the training structure, and the training calendar works backward from that date. The 16-week program is structured as four blocks that progress from general strength development through competition-specific practice to final peaking. Unlike powerlifting, strongman competitions vary significantly in their events — the program must be adjusted in Blocks 3–4 to reflect the specific events in the upcoming contest.

## Who This Is For

- Athletes with 2+ years of strongman training and competition experience
- Has completed `strongman_intermediate_block` or equivalent
- Competing in a specific contest on or around week 16
- Minimum baseline: Deadlift ≥ 2.5× BW, Overhead Press ≥ 1.2× BW, Back Squat ≥ 2× BW

## 16-Week Block Structure

| Block | Weeks | Character | Intensity |
|-------|-------|-----------|-----------|
| General Prep | 1–5 | Volume accumulation; all events | 70–80% |
| Specific Prep | 6–10 | Contest event emphasis; fewer events | 80–90% |
| Competition Specific | 11–14 | Contest events at contest weight | 90–100% |
| Peak/Taper | 15–16 | 50% volume; contest simulation; rest | Maintenance |

## Weekly Schedule

| Day | Focus |
|-----|-------|
| Mon | Deadlift strength + Back Squat (strength) |
| Tue | Overhead strength + Row |
| Wed | Squat volume + Front Squat |
| Thu | Event Day: Pulling (deadlift, carries, loading) |
| Fri | Event Day: Pressing + Yoke |
| Sat–Sun | Rest |

## Contest Event Integration (Blocks 3–4)

By Block 3, event training must match the specific contest implements and distances:

| Contest event type | Training simulation |
|-------------------|---------------------|
| Deadlift for reps | Max reps @ contest weight in 60 s |
| Log press for reps or max | Log press singles/reps @ contest weight |
| Farmer carry | Full distance + turn @ contest load |
| Yoke walk | Full course @ contest load |
| Atlas stone series | Stone-to-platform at each contest weight |
| Loading medley | Full medley simulation × 2 per week |

Event simulation must happen on the actual implements. If a log is not available before Block 2, acquire one before Block 3 begins.

## Competition Week Protocol

**Monday**: All contest implements at ≤50% of competition weight for single reps only. Technique confirmation — not effort work. Intensity at 50% of peak is the research consensus for the final training day before cessation (Winwood 2018).
**Tuesday–Thursday**: Rest or very light mobility work. No barbell. Complete training cessation 3–4 days before competition allows full neuromuscular restoration.
**Friday** (contest day – 1): Arrive at venue; practice warm-up routine; visualize openers.
**Saturday**: Competition.

**Attempt selection**: Open each event at 90% of best training performance, not best ever. Second attempts at 97–100%. Third attempts only for PRs when body and competition position justify the risk.

## Weight Class Management

Most strongman competitions have weight classes. If cutting:
- Stay within 5 kg of contest weight throughout the program
- Water cuts of more than 5 kg impair explosive event performance
- For same-day weigh-in: full rehydration is not possible; this is not powerlifting

See `hydration` for fluid replacement protocols. See `energy_balance` for contest prep caloric management.
