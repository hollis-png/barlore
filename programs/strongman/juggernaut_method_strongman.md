# Juggernaut Method Strongman

The Juggernaut Method was developed by Chad Wesley Smith, a powerlifting world champion and professional strongman coach, as a block periodization system that provides structure and predictable progression for athletes whose linear progression has stalled. Its adaptation for strongman introduces a parallel event-training periodization track that runs alongside the barbell strength work, with both tracks converging toward competition readiness in the final block.

The defining feature of the Juggernaut Method is the **AMRAP set**: on the third week of each 4-week block, the athlete performs one all-out set to maximize reps at the prescribed weight. This serves two purposes: it generates additional training volume while providing a direct 1RM prediction that drives loading for the next block.

## Who This Is For

- Intermediate athletes whose linear progression on barbell lifts has stalled
- Athletes with at least 6–12 months of strongman training and 1–2 competitions
- Athletes with consistent access to all competition implements
- Minimum baseline: Deadlift ≥ 2× BW, Overhead Press ≥ 0.9× BW, Squat ≥ 1.75× BW

## 4-Block Structure

| Block | Weeks | Barbell Rep Target | Event Character | Deload Week |
|-------|-------|-------------------|-----------------|-------------|
| Block 1 | 1–4 | 10RM wave | Volume accumulation; technical event work | Week 4 |
| Block 2 | 5–8 | 8RM wave | Increasing event load; tacky introduced | Week 8 |
| Block 3 | 9–12 | 5RM wave | Event loads near competition; competition simulation begins | Week 12 |
| Block 4 | 13–16 | 3RM wave | Barbell volume reduced; event simulation dominant; taper | Week 16 |

## 4-Day Weekly Schedule

| Day | Focus |
|-----|-------|
| Monday | Deadlift + Squat + Posterior chain |
| Tuesday | Overhead Press + Pull-ups |
| Wednesday | Squat + Events (stones, front squat) |
| Thursday | Log + Farmer + Yoke + Pull-ups |

## Juggernaut Wave Scheme (Barbell Lifts)

| Week in Block | Sets | Reps | Load |
|---------------|------|------|------|
| Week 1 | 3 | 10/8/5/3 | 60% 1RM for target rep count + 25 kg |
| Week 2 | 3 | 10/8/5/3 | +5 kg |
| Week 3 | 3 | 10/8/5/3 + AMRAP | +5 kg (go all out on AMRAP set) |
| Week 4 | Deload | — | 50% volume; same intensity |

*Rep count in the wave header indicates the block's target (Block 1 = 10, Block 2 = 8, etc.)*

## Event Periodization by Block

| Block | Farmer Carry | Atlas Stone | Yoke | Log |
|-------|-------------|-------------|------|-----|
| Block 1 | 4 × 20 m at 50% | 3–5 loads/session, no tacky | 3 × 15 m, light | 5 × 5 at 60% |
| Block 2 | 3 × 20 m at 65% | Full series at 70%, tacky | 3 × 15 m at 70% | 4 × 3 at 75% |
| Block 3 | 3 × full distance at 80% | Full series at 85%, tacky | 2 × full distance at 85% | 3 × 2 at 85% |
| Block 4 | 2 × full distance at competition weight | Full series at competition weight | 2 × full distance at comp weight | Singles at competition weight |

## Key Rules

- **The AMRAP set is mandatory.** Never cap the reps on the AMRAP set. The final set is the most valuable data point in the entire block — it predicts your 1RM and drives the next block's loading.
- **Both tracks must progress.** If event training is stalling but barbell work is progressing, reduce the barbell block intensity temporarily and prioritize the event. The competition is decided on implements, not barbells.
- **Deload weeks are non-negotiable.** The fourth week of each block is a deload. Juggernaut's wave loading strategy depends on the deload week to allow supercompensation before the next block's loading increase.
