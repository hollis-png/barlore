# Invictus Athlete

CJ Martin's Invictus Athlete program is built on a 16-week periodized block structure that explicitly separates training phases by dominant energy system — a more systematic approach to concurrent training than the daily-mixed format used by most CrossFit programs. The program is organized around the principle that aerobic adaptation and strength adaptation cannot be optimally pursued simultaneously within the same short training period; Invictus dedicates entire 4-week blocks to one quality at a time, while maintaining all others at minimum effective dose.

## Who This Is For

- Advanced CrossFit athletes competing in the Open and aiming for sanctional qualifying (top 5%)
- Athletes who have specific identifiable weaknesses to target (aerobic base, strength, gymnastics)
- Prerequisites: Back squat ≥ 2.0× BW, Clean & jerk ≥ 1.0× BW, Snatch ≥ 0.8× BW, Fran ≤ 4:30, ring muscle-up established
- Two-a-day training capacity: minimum 4–6 hours between sessions

## 16-Week Block Structure

**Block 1 (Weeks 1–4): Aerobic Foundation**
- AM: Long Zone 2 aerobic sessions (30–45 min row/run/bike) + strict gymnastics
- PM: Strength accumulation (5×5) + aerobic threshold metcons (15–22 min)
- Goal: Raise aerobic ceiling — every other quality is supported by oxidative base

**Block 2 (Weeks 5–8): Strength Accumulation**
- AM: Olympic lifting technique + interval conditioning (4×5 min at threshold)
- PM: Heavy strength (5×4 at 75–85%) + moderate metcons (10–18 min)
- Goal: Build strength reserve to reduce metcon economy cost

**Block 3 (Weeks 9–12): Sport-Specific Intensification**
- AM: Olympic lifting heavy singles + lactic sprint capacity (30–90 sec max efforts)
- PM: Near-max strength (5×3 at 82–90%) + Open-format WODs (2–12 min)
- Goal: Peak lactic power and sport-specific skill under fatigue

**Block 4 (Weeks 13–16): Competition Peak**
- AM: Reduced volume; skill maintenance only
- PM: Taper; 90–95% intensity on all main lifts; competition-format WODs only
- Goal: Express fitness; arrive at competition at peak with cleared fatigue

## Weekly Schedule (Block 2 Example)

| Session | AM | PM |
|---------|----|----|
| Monday | Clean & jerk technique (5 singles at 82%) | Back squat 5×4 at 78% + Long metcon |
| Tuesday | Snatch technique + row intervals | Deadlift 4×3 at 83% + Short metcon |
| Wednesday | Gymnastics skill (muscle-ups, HSPU) | Back squat 5×4 at 80% + Threshold WOD |
| Thursday | Snatch cycling + aerobic run | C&J heavy + Overhead press + Metcon |
| Friday | Gymnastics + pull-up volume | Back squat 5×4 at 82% + Sprint WOD |
| Saturday | Long row (40 min aerobic) or benchmark test | Rest |
| Sunday | Rest | Rest |

## Benchmark Testing (Start and End of Each Block)

| Benchmark | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| 2K Row | Aerobic power indicator |
| Fran | Lactic/strength integration |
| Max unbroken butterfly pull-ups | Gymnastics capacity |
| Heavy power clean (1RM) | Barbell cycling ceiling |
| 1RM back squat | Strength reserve |

## Progression

- **Strength**: Undulating load within each block; increases 2–3% per week within block
- **Gymnastics**: Progress one tier per block (strict → kipping → weighted or deficit)
- **Energy systems**: Shift dominant system priority by block; maintain others at MEV
