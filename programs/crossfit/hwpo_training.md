# HWPO (Hard Work Pays Off)

Created by Mat Fraser — five-time CrossFit Games Champion — HWPO is structured around a two-a-day framework that separates aerobic / gymnastics work (AM) from strength and high-intensity conditioning (PM). The guiding principle is that the AMPK pathway activated by aerobic conditioning in the AM must return to baseline before the PM session stimulates mTOR-dependent strength and hypertrophy adaptations; a minimum 4–6 hour gap (8 hours optimal) between sessions is non-negotiable.

## Who This Is For

- Advanced CrossFit athletes aiming at Open top 10% placement or sanctional qualification
- Athletes who have completed an intermediate program and have established gymnastics fundamentals (kipping pull-ups, toes-to-bar, kipping HSPU, double-unders)
- Prerequisites: Back squat ≥ 2.0× BW, Power clean ≥ 1.0× BW, Fran ≤ 5:00

## Weekly Schedule

| Session | Day | AM Focus | PM Focus |
|---------|-----|----------|----------|
| Day 1 | Monday | Aerobic row + gymnastics (pull-ups, HSPU) | Back squat + Olympic + Metcon |
| Day 2 | Tuesday | Zone 2 run/row + snatch technique | Clean & jerk + Deadlift + Metcon |
| Day 3 | Wednesday | Aerobic row + gymnastics (rings, TTB) | Back squat + Olympic + Metcon |
| Day 4 | Thursday | Zone 2 bike/row + C&J technique | Overhead press + Deadlift + Metcon |
| Day 5 | Friday | Aerobic row + gymnastics (HSPU, pull-ups) | Back squat + Olympic + Metcon |
| — | Saturday | Active recovery or long aerobic (60–90 min easy row) | — |
| — | Sunday | Full rest | — |

## Block Periodization

**Block 1 (Weeks 1–4) — Aerobic Base & Strength Accumulation**

| Component | Volume/Intensity |
|-----------|-----------------|
| AM aerobic | 20–40 min Zone 2 daily |
| Strength (Squat, DL) | 5×5 at 70–80% 1RM |
| Olympic lifting | Technique focus 70–80% |
| Metcon time domain | 12–20 min aerobic threshold |

**Block 2 (Weeks 5–8) — Threshold & Strength Intensification**

| Component | Volume/Intensity |
|-----------|-----------------|
| AM aerobic | 15–25 min + 1–2 interval sessions/week |
| Strength | 5×3 at 80–90% 1RM |
| Olympic lifting | Heavy singles 85–90% |
| Metcon time domain | 6–12 min lactic threshold |

**Block 3 (Weeks 9–12) — Open Peaking & Realization**

| Component | Volume/Intensity |
|-----------|-----------------|
| AM aerobic | 10–20 min; reduce volume 40% |
| Strength | 3×1–2 at 90–97% 1RM |
| Olympic lifting | Competition singles |
| Metcon time domain | 2–8 min peak intensity |

## Sample WOD Formats (PM Metcons)

**Sprint (Lactic, 3–6 min):**
"Grace" — 30 Clean & Jerks for time @ 60/43 kg

**Threshold (8–15 min):**
5 rounds: 10 Toes-to-Bar + 10 Wall Balls + 10 Box Jumps

**Aerobic Chipper (18–25 min):**
For time: 1K Row → 50 Double-Unders → 30 Pull-ups → 20 Power Cleans (70/47 kg) → 10 Bar Muscle-ups

## Progression

- **Strength**: Wave loading across blocks (accumulation → intensification → realization)
- **Benchmark WODs**: Re-test same benchmark every 4 weeks; time or score improvement = progression
- **Two-a-day recovery signal**: If PM session quality drops on >2 consecutive days, reduce AM session duration by 30%; restore PM quality before resuming AM volume
