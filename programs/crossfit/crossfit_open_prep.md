# CrossFit Open Prep Cycle

This 8-week peaking cycle is designed to be inserted immediately before the CrossFit Open begins. It does not introduce new training stimuli — the goal is to *express* existing fitness, eliminate accumulated fatigue, and sharpen the specific skills and WOD formats that the Open demands. Athletes who attempt to get fitter during Open prep tend to arrive at competition week overtrained rather than peaked.

## Who This Is For

- Intermediate to advanced CrossFit athletes (6+ months of structured training) who want to peak specifically for the Open
- Athletes who have been training consistently through an off-season or base-building program for 3–6 months prior
- Not for: athletes just starting CrossFit or who do not have basic gymnastics skills established

## 8-Week Structure

| Phase | Weeks | Volume | Intensity | Focus |
|-------|-------|--------|-----------|-------|
| Intensification | 1–3 | High | 90–100% | Open-format WODs, skill sharpening |
| Peak | 4–5 | Moderate-high | 100% | Competition simulation, benchmark PRs |
| Taper | 6–7 | Low (−40%) | Maintained | Fatigue clearance, no new stimuli |
| Competition | 8 | Minimal | High (2 sessions) | Rest and activate before Open begins |

## Open WOD Movement Priority List

Based on analysis of Open workouts from 2011–2024, the following movements appear most frequently and should receive specific prep:

| Movement | Open Frequency | Prep Priority |
|----------|---------------|---------------|
| Pull-ups / C2B pull-ups | Very High | Critical |
| Double-unders | Very High | Critical |
| Wall balls | Very High | Critical |
| Toes-to-bar | High | Critical |
| Thrusters | High | Critical |
| Rowing | Moderate-High | Important |
| Power cleans / barbell cycling | Moderate-High | Important |
| Handstand push-ups | Moderate | Important |
| Box jumps / box jump-overs | Moderate | Standard |
| Muscle-ups (bar or ring) | Low-Moderate | Advanced only |

## Weekly Schedule (Weeks 1–3: Intensification)

| Day | Session Content |
|-----|----------------|
| Monday | Back squat maintenance (3×3 at 90%) + Open sprint WOD (3–7 min) |
| Tuesday | Gymnastics skill block (TTB + pull-ups + HSPU) + threshold WOD (10–16 min) |
| Wednesday | Barbell cycling (power clean + thruster) + Open AMRAP simulation (20 min) |
| Thursday | Strength maintenance (press + deadlift, 2×3 each at 88%) + sprint WOD |
| Friday | Full Open simulation: announce, prep, execute as competition, score |
| Saturday/Sunday | Rest or light aerobic (30 min Zone 2) |

## Competition Week (Week 8)

- **Monday**: Light row (15 min easy aerobic) + 3×10 each: pull-ups, TTB, DUs — activation only
- **Tuesday**: Power clean singles at 80% 1RM (5 singles) + 2 short Open-paced sets — prime the system
- **Wednesday–Thursday**: Full rest
- **Friday**: Open Workout 1

## Taper Rules

- Volume reduction: −40% working sets Weeks 6–7 (maintain frequency)
- Intensity: unchanged — every set at same load/effort as Weeks 4–5
- No new movements or new stimuli in Weeks 6–8
- Nutrition: increase carbohydrates by 10–15% in Week 7 for glycogen supercompensation
