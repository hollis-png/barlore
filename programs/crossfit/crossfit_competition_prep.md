# CrossFit Competition Preparation

Advanced CrossFit programming assumes the athlete has mastered fundamental movement patterns and is targeting sanctioned competition (CrossFit Open, Sanctionals, or regional events). Training is organized into 16-week blocks with two-a-day sessions on most days — AM for strength and skill work, PM for conditioning. The program develops all four CrossFit fitness domains simultaneously: maximal strength, metabolic conditioning, gymnastic capacity, and Olympic lifting efficiency.

## Who This Is For

- Athletes competing in CrossFit Open or higher-level sanctioned events
- Minimum baselines: Snatch ≥ 0.9× BW, Clean & Jerk ≥ 1.1× BW, Back Squat ≥ 1.5× BW
- Capable of: 20+ unbroken pull-ups, 10+ ring dips, consistent double-unders
- Can handle and recover from two daily training sessions

## 16-Week Block Structure

| Block | Weeks | Strength Focus | Conditioning |
|-------|-------|---------------|-------------|
| Accumulation | 1–5 | 3–5 reps @ 75–85% | Long, aerobic-dominant WODs |
| Intensification | 6–10 | 1–3 reps @ 85–95% | Mixed intensity, benchmark testing |
| Peaking | 11–14 | Max testing | Competition simulation |
| Taper | 15–16 | 50% volume, maintain intensity | Short, sharp competition-pace WODs |

## Daily Structure (Two-a-Day)

**AM Session (90 min)**:
1. Warm-up + mobility (15 min)
2. Strength or Olympic lift work (30–40 min)
3. Skill gymnastics (20–25 min)
4. Short conditioning piece (10–15 min)

**PM Session (60–75 min)**:
1. Warm-up (10 min)
2. Primary conditioning WOD (20–45 min)
3. Accessory work (10–15 min)

Minimum 4–6 hours between AM and PM sessions.

## Weekly Template

| Day | AM | PM |
|-----|----|----|
| Mon | Back Squat + Snatch technique | Pull-up capacity + metcon |
| Tue | Deadlift + Overhead Press | Clean & Jerk cycling + metcon |
| Wed | Snatch (heavy singles) + Front Squat | Gymnastics skill + benchmark |
| Thu | Clean & Jerk (heavy) + Deadlift | Hero WOD or long chipper |
| Fri | Back Squat + Overhead Squat + Snatch | Short, heavy couplet |
| Sat | Clean & Jerk + Ring Dips | Aerobic Zone 2 (45–60 min) |
| Sun | Rest | |

## Competition Week Protocol

Weeks 15–16: 50% volume reduction. Keep all intensity cues (same loads, same gymnastics standards). Short, sharp workouts only. Practice competition movements at competition weight (not max effort). Full rest 2 days before competition.
