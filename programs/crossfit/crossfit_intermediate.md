# CrossFit Intermediate Strength & Conditioning

A structured 5-day program for athletes who have completed CrossFit Foundations and developed basic movement patterns. The intermediate stage shifts from learning movements to developing *capacity* within them: increasing strength on barbell lifts, building higher-rep gymnastics capacity, and improving metabolic conditioning benchmarks over time.

## Who This Is For

- Athletes who have completed CrossFit Foundations or 3–6 months of regular CrossFit
- Can perform: kipping pull-ups, basic barbell cycling (power clean, overhead press), double-unders
- Wants structured progression rather than randomized daily programming

## Weekly Schedule

| Day | Strength | Conditioning |
|-----|----------|-------------|
| Mon | Back Squat 5×3 + Ring Dips 3×max | AMRAP 12: Pull-ups / Pushups / Air Squats |
| Tue | Overhead Press 5×3 + Power Clean 5×3 | Couplet or triplet, 10–15 min |
| Wed | Conventional Deadlift 5×3 + Romanian DL 3×8 | Chipper or hero WOD (20–30 min) |
| Thu | Front Squat 4×4 + Back Squat 3×10 | EMOM 20: mixed monostructural |
| Fri | Push Press 5×3 + Power Clean cycling | Benchmark (5 rounds for time) |
| Sat–Sun | Rest or aerobic Zone 2 (see `aerobic_base`) | |

## Strength Work Protocol

Strength blocks precede conditioning each day. Warm up to working weight, complete all prescribed strength sets at the given percentage, then transition directly to conditioning after a short rest. This order ensures strength quality is not compromised by prior fatigue.

## Conditioning Structure

Each week includes:
- **1 AMRAP** (as many rounds as possible): tracks total work capacity
- **1 Benchmark WOD** (Girls, Heroes, or named workout): trackable over time
- **1 Chipper or long WOD**: engine work, sustained output
- **1 EMOM** (every minute on the minute): power output consistency
- **1 Couplet/Triplet**: high-intensity short effort

Keep conditioning scores. Re-test the same benchmark every 4 weeks to track improvement.

## Gymnastics Development

Gymnastics capacity grows from strict to kipping to butterfly on pull-ups, and from basic ring support to ring dips to muscle-up progressions. Prioritize:
1. Strict capacity before adding kipping volume
2. Ring dips before ring muscle-up attempts
3. Hollow body position as the foundation for all gymnastics

## Benchmark Targets at Program Completion

| Movement | Entry standard | 12-week goal |
|----------|---------------|-------------|
| Back Squat | 1.0× BW | 1.15× BW |
| Strict Press | 0.55× BW | 0.65× BW |
| Power Clean | 0.75× BW | 0.85× BW |
| Pull-ups (unbroken) | 10 | 15+ |
| Ring Dips | 5 | 10+ |
