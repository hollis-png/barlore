# Progressive Overload

Progressive overload is the systematic increase of training stimulus over time, so the body continually faces demands beyond its current level of adaptation.

## Core Definition

If the training stimulus stays constant, the body stops producing new structural change once it has adapted. The essence of progressive overload is keeping adaptation perpetually a step behind the stimulus.

## Ways to Apply Overload

- Increase load (most direct)
- Increase sets or total training volume
- Shorten rest intervals
- Improve technical quality (more efficient work at the same load)
- Increase exercise difficulty or range of motion

## How Systems Differ

Each system defines "more stimulus" differently. Those differences are recorded in each system's `index.md`; this entry defines only the principle itself.
