# Bodybuilding

Bodybuilding trains for maximum muscle size, symmetry, and definition rather than performance. Training is organized to accumulate sufficient volume per muscle group while managing fatigue, with exercise selection driven by which muscles a movement stimulates.

## Defining Characteristics

- Moderate-rep, moderate-to-high volume is the foundation
- Exercise selection prioritizes target-muscle stimulation
- Weekly volume per muscle is the primary progression variable
- Training often goes close to failure (low RIR)
