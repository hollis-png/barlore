# Strongman

Strongman competition tests absolute strength across a wide variety of events: log presses, atlas stone loads, axle deadlifts, frame carries, yoke walks, truck pulls, and more. No two competitions are identical — the event list changes, weights vary, and the athlete must be prepared for novelty. This makes strongman the most general-preparation strength sport.

## Defining Principles

- **Event specificity**: Unlike the fixed three lifts of powerlifting, strongman athletes must train for whatever events appear in upcoming competitions. Programming rotates event training frequently and prioritizes events on the upcoming roster.
- **Brute absolute strength**: Strongman rewards the largest absolute force output regardless of body weight class (though weight classes exist in some federations). Total body strength, not movement efficiency, is the primary determinant of performance.
- **Loaded carries as a distinct quality**: Farmer's walks, yoke carries, and sandbag carries train a form of strength-endurance under load that no other barbell sport emphasizes. The stabilization demands on the spine and hips under a moving load are unique.
- **Grip and odd-object handling**: Axle bars, logs, atlas stones, and kegs require grip strength and the ability to handle non-standard objects. These qualities are trained explicitly and directly.
- **High injury risk management**: The extreme loads and odd movement patterns in strongman events carry significant injury risk. Volume for event movements is carefully managed; general barbell strength is the base that reduces event injury risk.

## What Distinguishes Strongman from Other Systems

Strongman is the only major strength sport without a fixed movement set. This requires athletes to maintain broad strength across multiple patterns simultaneously, unlike powerlifting (three lifts) or weightlifting (two lifts). The loaded carry events and odd-object work have no equivalent in other barbell sports. Athletes must also develop the ability to repeat near-maximal efforts over a multi-event competition day.
