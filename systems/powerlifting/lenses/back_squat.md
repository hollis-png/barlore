# Powerlifting Lens: Back Squat

Powerlifting rules require the hip crease to drop below the top of the knee (below parallel), so technique choices center on maximizing the load that can be moved.

## Technical Approach

Powerlifters generally use a **low bar position**, because the shorter moment arm allows greater forward lean and recruits hips and legs together rather than relying on the quads alone. Stance is typically wider than the bodybuilding version, shortening the range of motion and improving the hip's leverage.

## Intensity Ranges

| Goal | Intensity (%1RM) | Sets × Reps |
|------|------------------|-------------|
| Max strength | 85–95% | 3–5 × 1–3 |
| Strength development | 75–85% | 4–6 × 3–5 |
| Technical accumulation | 65–75% | 5–8 × 5–8 |

## Periodization

Powerlifting squat work is usually organized as **percentage-based linear** or **conjugate** programming, ramping over 12–16 weeks before a meet from high-volume low-intensity toward a low-volume high-intensity peak. See `core/periodization.md` for the underlying principle.

## Common Meet Failures

- Insufficient depth (most common cause of a no-lift)
- Pausing partway up
- Bar path drifting forward
