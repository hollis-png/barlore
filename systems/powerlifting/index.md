# Powerlifting

Powerlifting is the sport of lifting the maximum possible load for one repetition in three competition lifts: the squat, bench press, and deadlift. Training is organized entirely around increasing one-rep-max strength in those three movements.

## Defining Characteristics

- Low-rep, high-intensity work is the foundation
- Technique is optimized for leverage, not muscle isolation
- Periodization peaks strength for a meet date
- Progress is measured by the competition total
