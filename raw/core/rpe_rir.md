## Sources

### Source 1
- Title: "The Reactive Training Manual"
- Author(s): Mike Tuchscherer
- Year: 2008
- Publisher: Reactive Training Systems
- URL: https://www.reactivetrainingsystems.com
- Credibility: practitioner
- Key facts extracted:
  - Adapted Borg RPE scale (originally 6–20) to a 1–10 scale for powerlifting
  - RPE 10 = maximal effort, could not have done another rep
  - RPE 9 = could have done 1 more rep
  - RPE 8 = could have done 2 more reps
  - RPE 7 = could have done 3 more reps
  - This direct mapping: RPE = 10 − RIR
  - Used to auto-regulate training load based on daily readiness
- Limitations / caveats:
  - Designed for advanced powerlifters; accuracy lower in beginners

### Source 2
- Title: "Novel Resistance Training–Specific Rating of Perceived Exertion Scale
  Measuring Repetitions in Reserve"
- Author(s): Zourdos, Klemp, Dolan, Quiles, Schau, Jo, Helms, Esgro, Duncan,
  Moquin, Blanco
- Year: 2016
- Journal: Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research
- DOI: 10.1519/JSC.0000000000001007
- Credibility: rct
- Key facts extracted:
  - Validated the RIR-based RPE scale against actual repetition performance
  - Trained subjects were more accurate at predicting RIR than untrained
  - RIR estimates within 1 rep are achievable in trained individuals
  - Intraclass correlation coefficient (ICC) was high for trained but lower for untrained
- Limitations / caveats:
  - Only validated at moderate loads (not at true 1RM)
  - Accuracy degrades in very high rep ranges (15+)

### Source 3
- Title: "The RIR-Based RPE Scale" (Helms, Morgan, Valdez — 3DMJ)
- Author(s): Eric Helms, Andy Morgan, Andrea Valdez
- Year: 2015
- URL: https://3dmusclejourney.com/rpe/
- Credibility: practitioner
- Key facts extracted:
  - Popularized use of RIR as the primary metric rather than RPE number
  - RIR is more intuitive: "2 reps left in the tank" is clearer than "RPE 8"
  - Used widely in natural bodybuilding and intermediate/advanced strength training
- Limitations / caveats:
  - Practitioner consensus; not directly from controlled research
