## Sources

### Source 1
- Title: "Scientific Principles of Hypertrophy Training"
- Author(s): Mike Israetel, James Hoffmann, Melissa Davis
- Year: 2021 (2nd ed.)
- Publisher: Renaissance Periodization
- URL: https://rpstrength.com/products/hypertrophy-training-guide-renaissance-periodization
- Credibility: expert_consensus
- Key facts extracted:
  - MEV (Minimum Effective Volume): the least volume per week that produces measurable
    progress for a given muscle group. Below MEV, training maintains but does not grow.
  - MAV (Maximum Adaptive Volume): the volume range in which most hypertrophic adaptation
    occurs. MAV is a range, not a fixed number; it shifts upward as training advances.
  - MRV (Maximum Recoverable Volume): the highest volume from which recovery is still
    possible before the next session. Exceeding MRV produces fatigue greater than adaptation.
  - Landmark order: MEV < MAV < MRV
  - Example ranges (intermediate, general — vary widely by individual and muscle group):
    - Quads: MEV ~8 sets/week, MRV ~20 sets/week
    - Chest: MEV ~8 sets/week, MRV ~22 sets/week
    - Biceps: MEV ~6 sets/week, MRV ~20 sets/week
  - MV (Maintenance Volume): even lower than MEV; enough to maintain current muscle
    mass with minimal fatigue (relevant during peaking or deload phases)
- Limitations / caveats:
  - Exact numbers are illustrative; individual variation is large
  - MRV depends on exercise selection, proximity to failure, load, and recovery resources
  - Not peer-reviewed; derived from synthesis of research + coaching experience

### Source 2
- Title: "Mesocycle Construction for Hypertrophy" (RP YouTube / article series)
- Author(s): Mike Israetel
- Year: 2019–2023 (ongoing)
- URL: https://rpstrength.com/blogs/articles/
- Credibility: practitioner
- Key facts extracted:
  - MEV is the starting point for a hypertrophy mesocycle; first week sets = MEV
  - Volume is progressively increased week-over-week until approaching MRV by week 3–5
  - Deload is taken before the next mesocycle; volume resets to MEV or below
  - The MEV → MRV progression within a mesocycle is the defining structure of RP-style
    periodization
- Limitations / caveats:
  - This is a commercial product; the model is simplified for practical coaching

### Source 3 (supporting research basis)
- Title: "Dose-response relationship between weekly resistance training volume and
  increases in muscle mass: A systematic review and meta-analysis"
- Author(s): Schoenfeld, Ogborn, Krieger
- Year: 2017
- DOI: 10.1080/02640414.2016.1210197
- Credibility: meta_analysis
- Key facts extracted:
  - Higher weekly set volumes produce greater hypertrophy up to a point
  - 10+ sets/week per muscle group superior to fewer sets in most populations
  - Dose-response curve is real; very high volumes not studied (ethical/practical limits)
- Limitations / caveats:
  - Does not directly measure MEV/MRV; these are extrapolated constructs applied to the
    dose-response data
