## OBJECTIVE 2 — Evidence Enrichment for Intermediate OWL Program

The knowledge base contains a synthesized `olympic_weightlifting_intermediate` program with the following structure:
- 4 days/week (Mon heavy snatch, Tue heavy C&J, Thu snatch volume, Fri C&J volume)
- Front squat 3×/week, back squat 1×/week (total ≤4 squat days)
- Snatch pull and clean pull at 95–105% of lift 1RM
- 12 weeks in 3 blocks (accumulation → intensification → peaking)
- Daily max approach on heavy days with mandatory back-off sets

Find evidence to validate or correct these design decisions:

### Research Questions

1. **Optimal training frequency for intermediate OWL athletes**
   - Search: "Olympic weightlifting training frequency intermediate" OR "snatch clean jerk sessions per week optimal"
   - Target: any RCT or systematic review comparing 3 vs 4 vs 5 days/week for intermediate lifters

2. **Pull intensity: 95–105% vs heavier**
   - Search: "snatch pull load Olympic weightlifting" OR "clean pull intensity percentage 1RM"
   - Target: EMG or force plate data on pulls at different intensities

3. **Front squat priority over back squat in OWL**
   - Search: "front squat vs back squat Olympic weightlifting performance" OR "front squat clean receiving strength"
   - Target: any correlation data between front squat 1RM and clean 1RM

4. **Back-off set efficacy after daily max**
   - Search: "post-activation potentiation back-off sets strength" OR "daily maximum training volume distribution"
   - Target: any evidence on volume accumulation after heavy singles

5. **Block periodization for intermediate OWL**
   - Search: "block periodization weightlifting intermediate" OR "periodization comparison Olympic lifting"
   - Target: any comparison of block vs linear vs undulating for OWL athletes
