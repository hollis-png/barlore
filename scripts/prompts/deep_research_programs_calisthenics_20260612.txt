## OBJECTIVE 2 — Evidence Enrichment for Calisthenics Programs

The knowledge base contains synthesized programs and system guides with the following claims:

1. **Connective tissue adaptation timeline**: 6–18 months for planche/front lever progressions; tendons adapt 3–4× more slowly than muscle
2. **Gelatin + Vitamin C protocol**: referenced as "Shaw 2017 RCT" (15–20 g gelatin + 50–100 mg Vitamin C, 60 minutes pre-training)
3. **Skill practice requires CNS freshness**: skill sessions must follow a rest day; cannot be placed after consecutive heavy strength days
4. **Planche scapular protraction vs retraction distinction**: planche requires protraction, opposite to barbell pressing patterns
5. **One-arm pull-up progression danger**: eccentric OAP negatives cause extreme distal biceps tendon stress
6. **Never train skills to technical failure**: failed repetition reinforces incorrect motor pattern

Find evidence to validate, correct, or add nuance to each claim:

### Research Questions

1. **Gelatin + Vitamin C protocol (Shaw 2017 RCT)**
   - Search: "gelatin vitamin C collagen synthesis exercise Shaw 2017" OR "collagen supplement connective tissue recovery training"
   - Target: the specific Shaw et al. 2017 paper — confirm: journal, sample size, protocol details (dose, timing), findings
   - Also find any replication or contradicting studies published after 2017

2. **Tendon vs muscle adaptation timescales**
   - Search: "tendon adaptation training timeline weeks months" OR "connective tissue vs muscle hypertrophy rate" OR "tendon collagen synthesis rate"
   - Target: any systematic review or review paper on tendon adaptation rates compared to muscle

3. **CNS fatigue and motor skill learning timing**
   - Search: "motor skill learning fatigue interference" OR "skill acquisition CNS recovery" OR "motor learning timing rest"
   - Target: any study on the effect of prior physical fatigue on subsequent motor skill learning

4. **Planche scapular mechanics (protraction vs retraction)**
   - Search: "planche scapular position EMG serratus anterior" OR "gymnastics support position scapula protraction"
   - Target: any EMG or biomechanical study of scapular position in gymnastics support/planche

5. **Eccentric loading and tendon injury risk in pulling exercises**
   - Search: "eccentric pull-up distal biceps tendon" OR "eccentric loading tendinopathy risk" OR "one-arm pull-up injury"
   - Target: any injury epidemiology or tendon stress study relevant to heavy eccentric pulling

6. **Motor learning and technical failure / errorful learning**
   - Search: "errorful learning motor skill" OR "error during practice motor learning outcome" OR "failed repetitions skill acquisition"
   - Target: any motor learning study addressing whether training through failure helps or hurts skill acquisition
