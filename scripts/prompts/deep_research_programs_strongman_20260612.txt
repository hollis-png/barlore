## OBJECTIVE 2 — Evidence Enrichment for Strongman Programs

The knowledge base contains synthesized programs:
- `strongman_intermediate_block`: 4 days/week, linear, barbell + implements, 3-4 week mesocycles
- `strongman_competition_prep`: 16-week contest prep, block periodization, event simulation escalation
- System guides specifying: yoke overload ≤10m only, Continental Clean as separate skill, carry velocity threshold, tacky from 6-8 weeks pre-competition

Find evidence to validate or correct these design decisions:

### Research Questions

1. **Strongman event training frequency and transfer**
   - Search: "strongman training implement specificity" OR "carry event training frequency" OR "strongman competition preparation periodization"
   - Target: any controlled study or well-documented case study on strongman-specific periodization

2. **Yoke walk biomechanics and injury risk**
   - Search: "yoke walk biomechanics" OR "loaded carry spinal compression" OR "yoke walk knee stress"
   - Target: any biomechanical analysis of yoke walk, especially distance and load effects on patellar/lumbar stress

3. **Farmer carry velocity as readiness metric**
   - Search: "farmer carry speed performance" OR "loaded carry velocity competition" OR "grip strength farmer walk"
   - Target: any normative data on farmer carry times or velocity thresholds

4. **Atlas stone mechanics and tacky (lifting chalk/adhesive) effects**
   - Search: "Atlas stone lift biomechanics" OR "strongman stone lifting technique" OR "tacky adhesive Atlas stone"
   - Target: any published biomechanical or coaching analysis of Atlas stone lifting technique

5. **Competition taper for strength-power athletes**
   - Search: "strength sport taper protocol" OR "competition peaking strongman powerlifting" OR "deload pre-competition strength"
   - Target: any RCT or systematic review on taper protocols applicable to strength-power sports

6. **Continental Clean technique and axle bar deadlift**
   - Search: "Continental Clean technique axle bar" OR "log clean press technique analysis"
   - Target: any biomechanical or coaching analysis of the Continental Clean
