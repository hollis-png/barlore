# Clean Deadlift

## Execution

1. Begin standing with a barbell close to your shins. Your feet should be directly under
   your hips with your feet turned out slightly. Grip the bar with a double overhand
   grip or hook grip, about shoulder width apart. Squat down to the bar. Your spine
   should be in full extension, with a back angle that places your shoulders in front of
   the bar and your back as vertical as possible.
2. Begin by driving through the floor through the front of your heels. As the bar travels
   upward, maintain a constant back angle. Flare your knees out to the side to help keep
   them out of the bar's path.
3. After the bar crosses the knees, complete the lift by driving the hips into the bar
   until your hips and knees are extended.
