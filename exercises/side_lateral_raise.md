# Side Lateral Raise

## Execution

1. Pick a couple of dumbbells and stand with a straight torso and the dumbbells by your
   side at arms length with the palms of the hand facing you. This will be your starting
   position.
2. While maintaining the torso in a stationary position (no swinging), lift the dumbbells
   to your side with a slight bend on the elbow and the hands slightly tilted forward as
   if pouring water in a glass. Continue to go up until you arms are parallel to the
   floor. Exhale as you execute this movement and pause for a second at the top.
3. Lower the dumbbells back down slowly to the starting position as you inhale.
4. Repeat for the recommended amount of repetitions.
