# Hip Extension with Bands

## Execution

1. Secure one end of the band to the lower portion of a post and attach the other to one
   ankle.
2. Facing the attachment point of the band, hold on to the column to stabilize yourself.
3. Keeping your head and your chest up, move the resisted leg back as far as you can while
   keeping the knee straight.
4. Return the leg to the starting position.
