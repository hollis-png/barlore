# Goblet Squat

The goblet squat is a squat performed holding a single dumbbell or kettlebell at chest height. The front-loaded weight encourages an upright torso and makes it one of the best entry points for learning the squat pattern.

## Execution

1. Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell vertically against the chest
2. Stance about shoulder width, toes slightly out
3. Descend with an upright torso, elbows tracking inside the knees
4. Drive up through the mid-foot

## Common Faults

- **Heels rising** — limited ankle mobility
- **Rounding at the bottom** — descending past current mobility
- **Weight drifting from the chest** — strains the upper back

## Variations

| Variation | Key difference | Best for |
|-----------|----------------|----------|
| Tempo goblet | Slow eccentric | Mobility and control |
| Pause goblet | Pause at bottom | Bottom-position strength |
