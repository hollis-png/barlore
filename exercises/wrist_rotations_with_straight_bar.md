# Wrist Rotations with Straight Bar

## Execution

1. Hold a barbell with both hands and your palms facing down; hands spaced about shoulder
   width. This will be your starting position.
2. Alternating between each of your hands, perform the movement by extending the wrist as
   though you were rolling up a newspaper. Continue alternating back and forth until
   failure.
3. Reverse the motion by flexing the wrist, rolling the opposite direction. Continue the
   alternating motion until failure.
