# Prone Manual Hamstring

## Execution

1. You will need a partner for this exercise. Lay face down with your legs straight. Your
   assistant will place their hand on your heel.
2. To begin, flex the knee to curl your leg up. Your partner should provide resistance,
   starting light and increasing the pressure as the movement is completed. Communicate
   with your partner to monitor appropriate resistance levels.
3. Pause at the top, returning the leg to the starting position as your partner provides
   resistance going the other direction.
