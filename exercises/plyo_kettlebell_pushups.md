# Plyo Kettlebell Pushups

## Execution

1. Place a kettlebell on the floor. Place yourself in a pushup position, on your toes with
   one hand on the ground and one hand holding the kettlebell, with your elbows
   extended. This will be your starting position.
2. Begin by lowering yourself as low as you can, keeping your back straight.
3. Quickly and forcefully reverse direction, pushing yourself up to the other side of the
   kettlebell, switching hands as you do so. Continue the movement by descending and
   repeating the movement back and forth.
