# Band Skull Crusher

## Execution

1. Secure a band to the base of a rack or the bench. Lay on the bench so that the band is
   lined up with your head.
2. Take hold of the band, raising your elbows so that the upper arm is perpendicular to the
   floor. With the elbow flexed, the band should be above your head. This will be your
   starting position.
3. Extend through the elbow to straighten your arm, keeping your upper arm in place. Pause
   at the top of the motion, and return to the starting position.
