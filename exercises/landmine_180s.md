# Landmine 180's

## Execution

1. Position a bar into a landmine or securely anchor it in a corner. Load the bar to an
   appropriate weight.
2. Raise the bar from the floor, taking it to shoulder height with both hands with your
   arms extended in front of you. Adopt a wide stance. This will be your starting
   position.
3. Perform the movement by rotating the trunk and hips as you swing the weight all the way
   down to one side. Keep your arms extended throughout the exercise.
4. Reverse the motion to swing the weight all the way to the opposite side.
5. Continue alternating the movement until the set is complete.
