# Bear Crawl Sled Drags

## Execution

1. Wearing either a harness or a loose weight belt, attach the chain to the back so that
   you will be facing away from the sled. Bend down so that your hands are on the
   ground. Your back should be flat and knees bent. This is your starting position.
2. Begin by driving with legs, alternating left and right. Use your hands to maintain
   balance and to help pull. Try to keep your back flat as you move over a given
   distance.
