# Squats - With Bands

## Execution

1. To start out, make sure that the exercise band is at an even split between both the left
   and right side of the body. To do this, use your hands to grab both sides of the band
   and place both feet in the middle of the band. Your feet should be shoulder width
   apart from each other.
2. When holding the bands, they should be the same height on each side. You should be using
   a pronated grip (palms facing forward) and have the handles of the bands next to your
   face for this exercise. This is the starting position.
3. Slowly start to bend the knees and lower the legs so that your thighs are parallel to
   the floor while exhaling.
4. Use the heel of your feet to push your body up to the starting position as you exhale.
5. Repeat for the recommended amount of repetitions.
