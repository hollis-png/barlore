# Two-Arm Kettlebell Clean

## Execution

1. Place two kettlebells between your feet. To get in the starting position, push your butt
   back and look straight ahead.
2. Clean the kettlebells to your shoulders by extending through the legs and hips as you
   raise the kettlebells towards your shoulders. Rotate your wrists as you do so.
3. Lower the kettlebells back to the starting position and repeat.
