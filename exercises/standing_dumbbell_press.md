# Standing Dumbbell Press

## Execution

1. Standing with your feet shoulder width apart, take a dumbbell in each hand. Raise the
   dumbbells to head height, the elbows out and about 90 degrees. This will be your
   starting position.
2. Maintaining strict technique with no leg drive or leaning back, extend through the elbow
   to raise the weights together directly above your head.
3. Pause, and slowly return the weight to the starting position.
