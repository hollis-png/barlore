# Bench Dips

## Execution

1. For this exercise you will need to place a bench behind your back. With the bench
   perpendicular to your body, and while looking away from it, hold on to the bench on
   its edge with the hands fully extended, separated at shoulder width. The legs will be
   extended forward, bent at the waist and perpendicular to your torso. This will be
   your starting position.
2. Slowly lower your body as you inhale by bending at the elbows until you lower yourself
   far enough to where there is an angle slightly smaller than 90 degrees between the
   upper arm and the forearm. Tip: Keep the elbows as close as possible throughout the
   movement. Forearms should always be pointing down.
3. Using your triceps to bring your torso up again, lift yourself back to the starting
   position.
4. Repeat for the recommended amount of repetitions.
