# Push Press - Behind the Neck

## Execution

1. Standing with the weight racked on the back of the shoulders, begin with the dip. With
   your feet directly under your hips, flex the knees without moving the hips backward.
   Go down only slightly, and reverse direction as powerfully as possible. Drive through
   the heels create as much speed and force as possible, moving the bar in a vertical
   path.
2. Using the momentum generated, finish pressing the weight overhead be extending through
   the arms.
3. Return to the starting position, using your legs to absorb the impact.
