# Bent Over Barbell Row

## Execution

1. Holding a barbell with a pronated grip (palms facing down), bend your knees slightly and
   bring your torso forward, by bending at the waist, while keeping the back straight
   until it is almost parallel to the floor. Tip: Make sure that you keep the head up.
   The barbell should hang directly in front of you as your arms hang perpendicular to
   the floor and your torso. This is your starting position.
2. Now, while keeping the torso stationary, breathe out and lift the barbell to you. Keep
   the elbows close to the body and only use the forearms to hold the weight. At the top
   contracted position, squeeze the back muscles and hold for a brief pause.
3. Then inhale and slowly lower the barbell back to the starting position.
4. Repeat for the recommended amount of repetitions.
