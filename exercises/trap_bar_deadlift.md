# Trap Bar Deadlift

## Execution

1. For this exercise load a trap bar, also known as a hex bar, to an appropriate weight
   resting on the ground. Stand in the center of the apparatus and grasp both handles.
2. Lower your hips, look forward with your head and keep your chest up.
3. Begin the movement by driving through the heels and extend your hips and knees. Avoid
   rounding your back at all times.
4. At the completion of the movement, lower the weight back to the ground under control.
