# Sandbag Load

## Execution

1. To load sandbags or other objects, begin with the implements placed a distance from the
   loading platform, typically 50 feet.
2. Begin by lifting the sandbag. Sandbags are extremely awkward, and the manner of lifting
   them can vary depending on the particular sandbag used. Reach as far around it as
   possible, extending through the hips and knees to pull it up high. Shouldering is
   usually not allowed.
3. Move as quickly as possible to the platform, and load it, extending through your hips,
   knees, and ankles to get it as high as possible. Place it onto the platform, ensuring
   it doesn't fall off.
4. Return to the starting position to retrieve the next sandbag, and repeat until the event
   is completed.
