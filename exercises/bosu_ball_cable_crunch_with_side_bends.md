# Bosu Ball Cable Crunch With Side Bends

## Execution

1. Connect a standard handle to each arm of a cable machine, and position them in the most
   downward position.
2. Grab a Bosu Ball and position it in front and center of the cable machine.
3. Lie down on the Bosu Ball with the small of your back arched around the ball. Your rear
   end should be close to the floor without touching it.
4. With both hands, reach back and grab the handle of each cable.
5. With your feet positioned in a wide stance, extend your arms straight out in front of
   you and in between your knees. Your hands should be at knee level.
6. Keep your arms straight and in-line with the upward angle of the cable. Elevate your
   torso in a crunching motion without dropping or bending your arms.
7. Maintain the rigid position with your arms. Slowly descend back to the starting position
   with your back arched around the Bosu Ball and your abdominals elongated.
8. Repeat the same series of movements to failure.
9. Once you reach failure, keep your abs tight and raise your torso into plank position so
   your back is elevated off the Bosu Ball.
10. Lower your arms down to your side; keep them straight. Start doing alternating side
   bends; reach for your heels! This finishing movement will focus on your obliques.
