# Hanging Leg Raise

## Execution

1. Hang from a chin-up bar with both arms extended at arms length in top of you using
   either a wide grip or a medium grip. The legs should be straight down with the pelvis
   rolled slightly backwards. This will be your starting position.
2. Raise your legs until the torso makes a 90-degree angle with the legs. Exhale as you
   perform this movement and hold the contraction for a second or so.
3. Go back slowly to the starting position as you breathe in.
4. Repeat for the recommended amount of repetitions.
