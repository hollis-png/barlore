# Kettlebell Pistol Squat

## Execution

1. Pick up a kettlebell with two hands and hold it by the horns. Hold one leg off of the
   floor and squat down on the other.
2. Squat down by flexing the knee and sitting back with the hips, holding the kettlebell up
   in front of you.
3. Hold the bottom position for a second and then reverse the motion, driving through the
   heel and keeping your head and chest up.
4. Lower yourself again and repeat.
