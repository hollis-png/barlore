# Natural Glute Ham Raise

## Execution

1. Using the leg pad of a lat pulldown machine or a preacher bench, position yourself so
   that your ankles are under the pads, knees on the seat, and you are facing away from
   the machine. You should be upright and maintaining good posture.
2. This will be your starting position. Lower yourself under control until your knees are
   almost completely straight.
3. Remaining in control, raise yourself back up to the starting position.
4. If you are unable to complete a rep, use a band, a partner, or push off of a box to aid
   in completing a repetition.
