# Standing Concentration Curl

## Execution

1. Taking a dumbbell in your working hand, lean forward. Allow your working arm to hang
   perpendicular to the ground with the elbow pointing out. This will be your starting
   position.
2. Flex the elbow to curl the weight, keeping the upper arm stationary. At the top of the
   repetition, flex the biceps and pause.
3. Lower the dumbbell back to the starting position.
4. Repeat the movement for the prescribed amount of repetitions.
