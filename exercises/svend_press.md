# Svend Press

## Execution

1. Begin in a standing position.
2. Press two lightweight plates together with your hands. Hold the plates together close to
   your chest to create an isometric contraction in your chest muscles. Your fingers
   should be pointed forward. This is your starting position.
3. Squeeze the plates between your palms and extend your arms directly out in front of you
   in a controlled motion.
4. Pause at the top of the motion, and then slowly return to the starting position.
