# Double Kettlebell Snatch

## Execution

1. Place two kettlebells behind your feet. Bend your knees and sit back to pick up the
   kettlebells.
2. Swing the kettlebells between your legs forcefully and reverse the direction.
3. Drive through with your hips and lock the ketttlebells overhead in one uninterrupted
   motion.
