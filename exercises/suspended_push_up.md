# Suspended Push-Up

## Execution

1. Anchor your suspension straps securely to the top of a rack or other object.
2. Leaning into the straps, take a handle in each hand and move into a push-up plank
   position. You should be as close to parallel to the ground as you can manage with
   your arms fully extended, maintaining good posture.
3. Maintaining a straight, rigid torso, descend slowly by allowing the elbows to flex.
4. Continue until your elbows break 90 degrees, pausing before you extend to return to the
   starting position.
