# Cocoons

## Execution

1. Begin by lying on your back on the ground. Your legs should be straight and your arms
   extended behind your head. This will be your starting position.
2. To perform the movement, tuck the knees toward your chest, rotating your pelvis to lift
   your glutes from the floor. As you do so, flex the spine, bringing your arms back
   over your head to perform a simultaneous crunch motion.
3. After a brief pause, return to the starting position.
