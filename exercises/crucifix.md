# Crucifix

## Execution

1. In the crucifix, you statically hold weights out to the side for time. While the event
   can be practiced using dumbbells, it is best to practice with one of the various
   implements used, such as axes and hammers, as it feels different.
2. Begin standing, and raise your arms out to the side holding the implements. Your arms
   should be parallel to the ground. In competition, judges or sensors are used to let
   you know when you break parallel. Hold for as long as you can. Typically, the weights
   should be heavy enough that you fail in 30-60 seconds.
