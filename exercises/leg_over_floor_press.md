# Leg-Over Floor Press

## Execution

1. Lie on the floor with one kettlebell in place on your chest, holding it by the handle.
   Extend leg on working side over leg on non-working side.Your free arm can be extended
   out to your side for support.
2. Press the kettlebll into a locked out position.
3. Lower the weight until the elbow touches the ground, keeping the kettlebell above the
   elbow. Repeat for the desired number of repetitions.
