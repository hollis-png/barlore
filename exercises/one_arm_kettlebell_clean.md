# One-Arm Kettlebell Clean

## Execution

1. Place a kettlebell between your feet. As you bend down to grab the kettlebell, push your
   butt back and keep your eyes looking forward.
2. Clean the kettlebell to your shoulders by extending through the legs and hips as you
   raise the kettlebell towards your shoulder. The wrist should rotate as you do so.
3. Return the weight to the starting position.
