# Smith Machine Decline Press

## Execution

1. Position a decline bench in the rack so that the bar will be above your chest. Load an
   appropriate weight and take your place on the bench.
2. Rotate the bar to unhook it from the rack and fully extend your arms. Your back should
   be slightly arched and your shoulder blades retracted. This will be your starting
   position.
3. Begin the movement by flexing your arms, lowering the bar to your chest.
4. Pause briefly, and then extend your arms to push the weight back to the starting
   position.
5. After completing the desired number of repetitions, rotate the bar to rack the weight.
