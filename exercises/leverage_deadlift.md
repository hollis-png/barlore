# Leverage Deadlift

## Execution

1. Load the pins to an appropriate weight. Position yourself directly between the handles.
   Grasp the bottom handles with a comfortable grip, and then lower your hips as you
   take a breath. Look forward with your head and keep your chest up. This will be your
   starting position.
2. Return the weight to the starting position.
