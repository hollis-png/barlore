# Physioball Hip Bridge

## Execution

1. Lay on a ball so that your upper back is on the ball with your hips unsupported. Both
   feet should be flat on the floor, hip width apart or wider. This will be your
   starting position.
2. Begin by extending the hips using your glutes and hamstrings, raising your hips upward
   as you bridge.
3. Pause at the top of the motion and return to the starting position.
