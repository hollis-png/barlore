# One-Arm Overhead Kettlebell Squats

## Execution

1. Clean and press a kettlebell with one arm. Clean the kettlebell to your shoulder by
   extending through the legs and hips as you pull the kettlebell towards your shoulder.
   Rotate your wrist as you do so. Press the weight overhead by extending through the
   elbow.This will be your starting position.
2. Looking straight ahead and keeping a kettlebell locked out above you, flex the knees and
   hips and lower your torso between your legs, keeping your head and chest up.
3. Pause at the bottom position for a second before rising back to the top, driving through
   the heels of your feet.
