# Backward Drag

## Execution

1. Load a sled with the desired weight, attaching a rope or straps to the sled that you can
   hold onto.
2. Begin the exercise by moving backwards for a given distance. Leaning back, extend
   through the legs for short steps to move as quickly as possible.
