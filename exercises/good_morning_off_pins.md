# Good Morning off Pins

## Execution

1. Begin with a bar on a rack at about the same height as your stomach. Bend over
   underneath the bar and rack the bar across the rear of your shoulders as you would a
   power squat, not on top of your shoulders. At the proper height, you should be near
   parallel to the floor when bent over. Keep your back tight, shoulder blades pinched
   together, and your knees slightly bent. Keep your back arched and your cervical spine
   in proper alignment.
2. Begin the motion by extending through the hips with your glutes and hamstrings, and you
   are standing with the weight. Slowly lower the weight back to the pins returning to
   the starting position.
