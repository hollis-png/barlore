# Forward Drag with Press

## Execution

1. Attach a dual handled chain or rope attachment to the sled. You should be facing away
   from the sled, holding a handle in each hand.
2. Begin the movement by moving forward for one step. Leaning forward, extend through the
   legs and hips to move, pausing with each step to extend through the elbows, pressing
   your hands forward. Step forward until you return to the start position prepared to
   press.
