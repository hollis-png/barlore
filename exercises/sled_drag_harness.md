# Sled Drag - Harness

## Execution

1. To begin, load the sled with the desired weight and attach the pulling strap. You can
   pull with handles, use a harness, or attach the pulling strap to a weight belt.
2. Whether pulling forwards or backwards, lean in the direction of travel and progress by
   extending through the hips and knees.
