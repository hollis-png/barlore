# Kneeling High Pulley Row

## Execution

1. Select the appropriate weight using a pulley that is above your head. Attach a rope to
   the cable and kneel a couple of feet away, holding the rope out in front of you with
   both arms extended. This will be your starting position.
2. Initiate the movement by flexing the elbows and fully retracting your shoulders, pulling
   the rope toward your upper chest with your elbows out.
3. After pausing briefly, slowly return to the starting position.
