# Frankenstein Squat

## Execution

1. This drill teaches you the proper positioning of both the bar and your body during the
   clean and front squat.
2. Place the barbell on the front of the shoulders, releasing your grip and extending your
   arms out in front of you. The shoulders should be pushed forward to create a shelf,
   and the bar should be in contact with the throat. Ensure that you only move your
   shoulder blades forward; don't round the thoracic spine.
3. Squat by flexing the knees and hips, sitting in between your legs. Keep the torso
   upright, the arms up, and the shoulders forward, and the bar should stay in place. Go
   to the bottom of the squat, until your hamstrings contact your calves.
4. Return to the upright position by driving through the front of the heel and extending
   the knees and hips.
