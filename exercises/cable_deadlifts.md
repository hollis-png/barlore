# Cable Deadlifts

## Execution

1. Move the cables to the bottom of the towers and select an appropriate weight. Stand
   directly in between the uprights.
2. To begin, squat down be flexing your hips and knees until you can reach the handles.
3. After grasping them, begin your ascent. Driving through your heels extend your hips and
   knees keeping your hands hanging at your side. Keep your head and chest up throughout
   the movement.
4. After reaching a full standing position, Return to the starting position and repeat.
