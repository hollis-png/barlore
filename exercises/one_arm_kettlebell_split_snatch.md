# One-Arm Kettlebell Split Snatch

## Execution

1. Hold a kettlebell in one hand by the handle.
2. Squat towards the floor, and then reverse the motion, extending the hips, knees, and
   finally the ankles, to raise the kettlebell overhead.
3. After fully extending the body, descend into a lunge position to receive the weights
   overhead, one leg forward and one leg back. Ensure you drive through with your hips
   and lock the ketttlebells overhead in one uninterrupted motion.
4. Return to a standing position, holding the weight overhead, and bring the feet together.
   Lower the weight to return to the starting position.
