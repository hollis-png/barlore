# Advanced Kettlebell Windmill

## Execution

1. Clean and press a kettlebell overhead with one arm.
2. Keeping the kettlebell locked out at all times, push your butt out in the direction of
   the locked out kettlebell. Keep the non-working arm behind your back and turn your
   feet out at a forty-five degree angle from the arm with the kettlebell.
3. Lower yourself as far as possible.
4. Pause for a second and reverse the motion back to the starting position.
