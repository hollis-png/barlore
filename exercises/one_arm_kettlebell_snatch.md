# One-Arm Kettlebell Snatch

## Execution

1. Place a kettlebell between your feet. Bend your knees and push your butt back to get in
   the proper starting position.
2. Look straight ahead and swing the kettlebell back between your legs.
3. Immediately reverse the direction and drive through with your hips and knees,
   accelerating the kettlebell upward. As the kettlebell rises to your shoulder rotate
   your hand and punch straight up, using momentum to receive the weight locked out
   overhead.
