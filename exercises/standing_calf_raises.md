# Standing Calf Raises

## Execution

1. Adjust the padded lever of the calf raise machine to fit your height.
2. Place your shoulders under the pads provided and position your toes facing forward (or
   using any of the two other positions described at the beginning of the chapter). The
   balls of your feet should be secured on top of the calf block with the heels
   extending off it. Push the lever up by extending your hips and knees until your torso
   is standing erect. The knees should be kept with a slight bend; never locked. Toes
   should be facing forward, outwards or inwards as described at the beginning of the
   chapter. This will be your starting position.
3. Raise your heels as you breathe out by extending your ankles as high as possible and
   flexing your calf. Ensure that the knee is kept stationary at all times. There should
   be no bending at any time. Hold the contracted position by a second before you start
   to go back down.
4. Go back slowly to the starting position as you breathe in by lowering your heels as you
   bend the ankles until calves are stretched.
5. Repeat for the recommended amount of repetitions.
