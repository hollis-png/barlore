# Elevated Back Lunge

## Execution

1. Position a bar onto a rack at shoulder height loaded to an appropriate weight. Place a
   short, raised platform behind you.
2. Rack the bar onto your upper back, keeping your back arched and tight. Step onto your
   raised platform with both feet. This will be your starting position.
3. Begin by stepping backwards with one leg. Descend by flexing your hips and knees until
   your knee touches the floor.
4. Pause, and extend through the hips and knees to rise up, returning all the way to the
   starting position before alternating.
