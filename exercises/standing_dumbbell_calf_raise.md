# Standing Dumbbell Calf Raise

## Execution

1. Stand with your torso upright holding two dumbbells in your hands by your sides. Place
   the ball of the foot on a sturdy and stable wooden board (that is around 2-3 inches
   tall) while your heels extend off and touch the floor. This will be your starting
   position.
2. With the toes pointing either straight (to hit all parts equally), inwards (for emphasis
   on the outer head) or outwards (for emphasis on the inner head), raise the heels off
   the floor as you exhale by contracting the calves. Hold the top contraction for a
   second.
3. As you inhale, go back to the starting position by slowly lowering the heels.
4. Repeat for the recommended amount of times.
