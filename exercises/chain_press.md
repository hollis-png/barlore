# Chain Press

## Execution

1. Begin by connecting the chains to the cable handle attachments. Position yourself on the
   flat bench in the same position as for a dumbbell press. Your wrists should be
   pronated and arms perpendicular to the floor. This will be your starting position.
2. Lower the chains by flexing the elbows, unloading some of the chain onto the floor.
3. Continue until your elbow forms a 90 degree angle, and then reverse the motion by
   extending through the elbow to lockout.
