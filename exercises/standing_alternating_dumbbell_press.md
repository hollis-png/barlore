# Standing Alternating Dumbbell Press

## Execution

1. Stand with a dumbbell in each hand. Raise the dumbbells to your shoulders with your
   palms facing forward and your elbows pointed out. This will be your starting
   position.
2. Extend one arm to press the dumbbell straight up, keeping your off hand in place. Do not
   lean or jerk the weight during the movement.
3. After a brief pause, return the weight to the starting position.
4. Repeat for the opposite side, continuing to alternate between arms.
