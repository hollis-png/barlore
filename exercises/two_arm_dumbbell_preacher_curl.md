# Two-Arm Dumbbell Preacher Curl

## Execution

1. Grab a dumbbell with each arm and place the upper arms on top of the preacher bench or
   the incline bench. The dumbbell should be held at shoulder length. This will be your
   starting position.
2. As you breathe in, slowly lower the dumbbells until your upper arm is extended and the
   biceps is fully stretched.
3. As you exhale, use the biceps to curl the weights up until your biceps is fully
   contracted and the dumbbells are at shoulder height.
4. Squeeze the biceps hard for a second at the contracted position and repeat for the
   recommended amount of repetitions.
