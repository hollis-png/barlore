# Leverage Decline Chest Press

## Execution

1. Load an appropriate weight onto the pins and adjust the seat for your height. The
   handles should be near the bottom of the pectorals at the beginning of the motion.
   Your chest and head should be up and your shoulder blades retracted. This will be
   your starting position.
2. Press the handles forward by extending through the elbow.
3. After a brief pause at the top, return the weight just above the start position, keeping
   tension on the muscles by not returning the weight to the stops until the set is
   complete.
