# Lying Triceps Press

## Execution

1. Lie on a flat bench with either an e-z bar (my preference) or a straight bar placed on
   the floor behind your head and your feet on the floor.
2. Grab the bar behind you, using a medium overhand (pronated) grip, and raise the bar in
   front of you at arms length. Tip: The arms should be perpendicular to the torso and
   the floor. The elbows should be tucked in. This is the starting position.
3. As you breathe in, slowly lower the weight until the bar lightly touches your forehead
   while keeping the upper arms and elbows stationary.
4. At that point, use the triceps to bring the weight back up to the starting position as
   you breathe out.
5. Repeat for the recommended amount of repetitions.
