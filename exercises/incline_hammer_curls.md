# Incline Hammer Curls

## Execution

1. Seat yourself on an incline bench with a dumbbell in each hand. You should pressed
   firmly against he back with your feet together. Allow the dumbbells to hang straight
   down at your side, holding them with a neutral grip. This will be your starting
   position.
2. Initiate the movement by flexing at the elbow, attempting to keep the upper arm
   stationary.
3. Continue to the top of the movement and pause, then slowly return to the start position.
