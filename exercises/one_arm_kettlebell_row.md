# One-Arm Kettlebell Row

## Execution

1. Place a kettlebell in front of your feet. Bend your knees slightly and then push your
   butt out as much as possible as you bend over to get in the starting position. Grab
   the kettlebell and pull it to your stomach, retracting your shoulder blade and
   flexing the elbow. Keep your back straight. Lower and repeat.
