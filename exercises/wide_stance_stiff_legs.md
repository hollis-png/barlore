# Wide Stance Stiff Legs

## Execution

1. Begin with a barbell loaded on the floor. Adopt a wide stance, and then bend at the hips
   to grab the bar. Your hips should be as far back as possible, and your legs nearly
   straight. Keep your back straight, and your head and chest up. This will be your
   starting position.
2. Begin the movement be engaging the hips, driving them forward as you allow the arms to
   hang straight. Continue until you are standing straight up, and then slowly return
   the weight to the starting position. For successive reps, the weight need not touch
   the floor.
