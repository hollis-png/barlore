# Bench Press - With Bands

## Execution

1. Using a flat bench secure a band under the leg of the bench that is nearest to your
   head.
2. Once the band is secure, grab it by both handles and lie down on the bench.
3. Extend your arms so that you are holding the band handles in front of you at shoulder
   width.
4. Once at shoulder width, rotate your wrists forward so that the palms of your hands are
   facing away from you. This will be your starting position.
5. Bring down the handles slowly until your elbow forms a 90 degree angle. Keep full
   control at all times.
6. As you breathe out, bring the handles up using your pectoral muscles. Lock your arms in
   the contracted position, squeeze your chest, hold for a second and then start coming
   down slowly. Tip: It should take at least twice as long to go down than to come up.
7. Repeat the movement for the prescribed amount of repetitions of your training program.
