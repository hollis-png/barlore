# Middle Back Shrug

## Execution

1. Lie facedown on an incline bench while holding a dumbbell in each hand. Your arms should
   be fully extended hanging down and pointing towards the floor. The palms of your
   hands should be facing each other. This will be your starting position.
2. As you exhale, squeeze your shoulder blades together and hold the contraction for a full
   second. Tip: This movement is just like the reverse action of a hug, or trying to
   perform rear laterals as if you had no arms.
3. As you inhale go back to the starting position.
4. Repeat for the recommended amount of repetitions.
