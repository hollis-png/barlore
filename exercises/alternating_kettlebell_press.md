# Alternating Kettlebell Press

## Execution

1. Clean two kettlebells to your shoulders. Clean the kettlebells to your shoulders by
   extending through the legs and hips as you pull the kettlebells towards your
   shoulders. Rotate your wrists as you do so.
2. Press one directly overhead by extending through the elbow, turning it so the palm faces
   forward while holding the other kettlebell stationary .
3. Lower the pressed kettlebell to the starting position and immediately press with your
   other arm.
