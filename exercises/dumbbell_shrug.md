# Dumbbell Shrug

## Execution

1. Stand erect with a dumbbell on each hand (palms facing your torso), arms extended on the
   sides.
2. Lift the dumbbells by elevating the shoulders as high as possible while you exhale. Hold
   the contraction at the top for a second. Tip: The arms should remain extended at all
   times. Refrain from using the biceps to help lift the dumbbells. Only the shoulders
   should be moving up and down.
3. Lower the dumbbells back to the original position.
4. Repeat for the recommended amount of repetitions.
