# Russian Twist

## Execution

1. Lie down on the floor placing your feet either under something that will not move or by
   having a partner hold them. Your legs should be bent at the knees.
2. Elevate your upper body so that it creates an imaginary V-shape with your thighs. Your
   arms should be fully extended in front of you perpendicular to your torso and with
   the hands clasped. This is the starting position.
3. Twist your torso to the right side until your arms are parallel with the floor while
   breathing out.
4. Hold the contraction for a second and move back to the starting position while breathing
   out. Now move to the opposite side performing the same techniques you applied to the
   right side.
5. Repeat for the recommended amount of repetitions.
