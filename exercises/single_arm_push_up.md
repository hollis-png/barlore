# Single-Arm Push-Up

## Execution

1. Begin laying prone on the ground. Move yourself into a position supporting your weight
   on your toes and one arm. Your working arm should be placed directly under the
   shoulder, fully extended. Your legs should be extended, and for this movement you may
   need a wider base, placing your feet further apart than in a normal push-up.
2. Maintain good posture, and place your free hand behind your back. This will be your
   starting position.
3. Lower yourself by allowing the elbow to flex until you touch the ground.
4. Descend slowly, and reverse direction be extending the arm to return to the starting
   position.
