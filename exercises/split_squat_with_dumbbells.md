# Split Squat with Dumbbells

## Execution

1. Position yourself into a staggered stance with the rear foot elevated and front foot
   forward.
2. Hold a dumbbell in each hand, letting them hang at the sides. This will be your starting
   position.
3. Begin by descending, flexing your knee and hip to lower your body down. Maintain good
   posture througout the movement. Keep the front knee in line with the foot as you
   perform the exercise.
4. At the bottom of the movement, drive through the heel to extend the knee and hip to
   return to the starting position.
