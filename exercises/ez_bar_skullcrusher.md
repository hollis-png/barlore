# EZ-Bar Skullcrusher

## Execution

1. Using a close grip, lift the EZ bar and hold it with your elbows in as you lie on the
   bench. Your arms should be perpendicular to the floor. This will be your starting
   position.
2. Keeping the upper arms stationary, lower the bar by allowing the elbows to flex. Inhale
   as you perform this portion of the movement. Pause once the bar is directly above the
   forehead.
3. Lift the bar back to the starting position by extending the elbow and exhaling.
4. Repeat.
