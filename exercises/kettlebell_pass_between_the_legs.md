# Kettlebell Pass Between The Legs

## Execution

1. Place one kettlebell between your legs and take a comfortable stance. Bend over by
   pushing your butt out and keeping your back flat.
2. Pick up a kettlebell and pass it to your other hand between your legs, in the fashion of
   a "W". Go back and forth for several repetitions.
