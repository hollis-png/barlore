# Alternating Deltoid Raise

## Execution

1. In a standing position, hold a pair of dumbbells at your side.
2. Keeping your elbows slightly bent, raise the weights directly in front of you to
   shoulder height, avoiding any swinging or cheating.
3. Return the weights to your side.
4. On the next repetition, raise the weights laterally, raising them out to your side to
   about shoulder height.
5. Return the weights to the starting position and continue alternating to the front and
   side.
