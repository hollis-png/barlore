# Seated Barbell Military Press

## Execution

1. Sit on a Military Press Bench with a bar behind your head and either have a spotter give
   you the bar (better on the rotator cuff this way) or pick it up yourself carefully
   with a pronated grip (palms facing forward). Tip: Your grip should be wider than
   shoulder width and it should create a 90-degree angle between the forearm and the
   upper arm as the barbell goes down.
2. Once you pick up the barbell with the correct grip length, lift the bar up over your
   head by locking your arms. Hold at about shoulder level and slightly in front of your
   head. This is your starting position.
3. Lower the bar down to the collarbone slowly as you inhale.
4. Lift the bar back up to the starting position as you exhale.
5. Repeat for the recommended amount of repetitions.
