# Romanian Deadlift

## Execution

1. Put a barbell in front of you on the ground and grab it using a pronated (palms facing
   down) grip that a little wider than shoulder width. Tip: Depending on the weight
   used, you may need wrist wraps to perform the exercise and also a raised platform in
   order to allow for better range of motion.
2. Bend the knees slightly and keep the shins vertical, hips back and back straight. This
   will be your starting position.
3. Keeping your back and arms completely straight at all times, use your hips to lift the
   bar as you exhale. Tip: The movement should not be fast but steady and under control.
4. Once you are standing completely straight up, lower the bar by pushing the hips back,
   only slightly bending the knees, unlike when squatting. Tip: Take a deep breath at
   the start of the movement and keep your chest up. Hold your breath as you lower and
   exhale as you complete the movement.
5. Repeat for the recommended amount of repetitions.
