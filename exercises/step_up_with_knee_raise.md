# Step-up with Knee Raise

## Execution

1. Stand facing a box or bench of an appropriate height with your feet together. This will
   be your starting position.
2. Begin the movement by stepping up, putting your left foot on the top of the bench.
   Extend through the hip and knee of your front leg to stand up on the box. As you
   stand on the box with your left leg, flex your right knee and hip, bringing your knee
   as high as you can.
3. Reverse this motion to step down off the box, and then repeat the sequence on the
   opposite leg.
