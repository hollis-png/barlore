# Scapular Pull-Up

## Execution

1. Take a pronated grip on a pull-up bar.
2. From a hanging position, raise yourself a few inches without using your arms. Do this by
   depressing your shoulder girdle in a reverse shrugging motion.
3. Pause at the completion of the movement, and then slowly return to the starting position
   before performing more repetitions.
